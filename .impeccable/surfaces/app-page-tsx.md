# Surface Brief: Real Estate Sales Agent Call-Logging CRM (`app/page.tsx`)

- **Scope & Mode:** Core CRM web application (`Operate` mode, mobile-first 390×844 portrait primary surface expanding cleanly to desktop split dossier).
- **Approved Comp:** `.impeccable/mocks/comp-3.png`
- **Audience & Job:** Real estate sales agent logging every phone number called, preventing duplicate outreach, capturing call outcomes/feedback in under 10 seconds, and working through overdue and today's follow-ups.

## Direction contract

THESIS: Every real estate lead is a perforated Master Lead Coupon inside a jet-age dispatch wallet where call outcomes, duplicate checks, and follow-up schedules stamp themselves permanently onto numbered flight-leg stubs instead of hiding inside generic SaaS cards.

OWN-WORLD: Carrier Navy (`#0D1B3D`), Jet Red (`#D7262D`) for overdue banners and primary call-logging triggers, Carbon Purple (`#4B2E83`) for WhatsApp coupons and fixed data figures, Verification Emerald (`#1E6B52`) for non-duplicate phone stamps, Crisp Coupon White (`#FFFFFF`), and Stock White (`#F2F2F0`) with scalloped ticket notches, 1px hairline ruled spec boxes, and barcode call-leg stubs.

STORY: The agent sees overdue and due-today follow-up counts in the top Jet Red alert band, inspects or switches the active Master Lead Coupon (verifying duplicate-free phone numbers and 9 quick-change status pills), taps `CALL TEL:`, `WHATSAPP (EDIT MSG)`, or `+ LOG CALL (<10S)`, and records or reviews numbered Call Legs sorted newest-first.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Full-bleed Jet Red overdue alert strip at the top (`TODAY'S FOLLOW-UPS: 2 OVERDUE · 2 DUE TODAY — TAP TO SWITCH`), Carrier Navy ticket header (`MASTER LEAD COUPON · REAL ESTATE CRM`), Master Lead Coupon for `MARCUS VANCE` with `VERIFIED · NO DUPLICATE` stamp, primary/secondary phones, 2×3 hairline-ruled spec grid (`SOURCE`, `REQ`, `TYPE`, `AREA`, `BUDGET`, `ADDED`), 9-status quick-stamp selector ribbon, three perforated action tickets (`CALL TEL:`, `WHATSAPP (EDIT MSG)`, `+ LOG CALL (<10S)`), `GENERAL NOTES` box, and `CALL LOG & FEEDBACK TIMELINE` with barcode coupon stubs (`CALL LEG #04`, `#03`, `#02`).

FORM: Challenger 2 (`vernacular-ephemera-jet-age-ticket-wallet` — Jet-Age Coupon Dossier), fused with real estate call-desk workflow; seed key `bb614e8f`.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
